# Startup Bahrain Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 18 August 2026**

Startup Bahrain operates a Facebook Page that shares Bahrain job opportunities, public updates, and news from verified or official sources.

## Information We Collect

The Startup Bahrain Automation app is used only by the Page administrator to create and publish content to the Startup Bahrain Facebook Page. The app does not provide a public login service and does not intentionally collect personal information from Facebook users or Page visitors.

## Facebook and Meta Data

The automation uses Meta permissions required to manage and publish posts on the Startup Bahrain Facebook Page. Access credentials are used only for Page-management functions and are not sold or shared with advertisers or unrelated third parties.

## Storage and Security

Automation credentials are stored privately using protected repository secrets and are not displayed publicly. Startup Bahrain does not maintain a separate database of Facebook visitor data through this automation.

## Third-Party Links

Posts may contain links to official government websites, employers, career pages, or other verified sources. Those external websites have their own privacy practices and policies.

## Data Deletion

Because the Startup Bahrain Automation app does not intentionally store personal information from Facebook users, there is normally no user data held by the app to delete. If you believe information relating to you has been processed through Startup Bahrain, you may contact the Startup Bahrain Facebook Page and request review or deletion where applicable.

## Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated if the Startup Bahrain service or automation changes.

For privacy questions or requests, contact Startup Bahrain through its official Facebook Page:
